# Oncology and Stem Cell Transplantation for the Virologist

A free, open-access clinical companion: haematology, cancer therapy, cellular therapy and the haematopoietic stem cell transplant journey — written as notes for virologists who care for infections in cancer and transplant patients.

**Author:** Saranga Sumathipala · **Edition:** First edition, 2026 · **Format:** PDF, 95 pages

**[Download the PDF](Oncology_and_Stem_Cell_Transplantation_for_the_Virologist.pdf)** · **[Web page](https://gsumathipala.github.io/oncology-stem-cell-transplantation-for-virologists/)**

## Contents

- **Part I — Foundations:** haematopoiesis and the blood count; the immune system; how cancer develops, including viral oncogenesis; diagnosis, grading and staging.
- **Part II — Haematological malignancies:** acute leukaemias; MDS, MPN and CML; lymphomas and CLL; myeloma; non-malignant diseases treated by transplant.
- **Part III — Solid tumours:** what the virologist needs to know.
- **Part IV — Treatment and its consequences:** chemotherapy, radiotherapy, targeted drugs, antibodies, bispecifics, checkpoint inhibitors, CAR-T, supportive care, oncological emergencies.
- **Part V — Immunosuppression and infection:** the net state of immunosuppression; viral infections outside transplantation; vaccination.
- **Part VI — Stem cell transplantation:** indications; donors and HLA; mobilisation and collection; conditioning; Day 0 and aplasia; engraftment and chimerism; GVHD prophylaxis, acute and chronic GVHD; immune reconstitution; infections (CMV, EBV/PTLD, adenovirus, HHV-6, BK, respiratory viruses and others); non-infectious complications; relapse; late effects and revaccination; the transplant unit day by day.
- **Appendices:** abbreviations, laboratory values, drug glossary, grading systems, 47 references checked in PubMed.

27 original infographics and 13 openly licensed photographs and micrographs.
